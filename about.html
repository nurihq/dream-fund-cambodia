About Us

The story behind our mission and the values that drive us every day.

Our Story

It started with a single visit.

Dream Fund Cambodia was born from a simple trip to a rural village outside Phnom Penh. We saw children eager to learn but lacking even the most basic supplies — no pencils, no paper, nothing to write with.

We returned with bags full of stationery and the reaction was unforgettable. Children who had never held a crayon before lit up with pure, uncontainable joy. Teachers who had been writing on broken chalkboards suddenly had tools to work with.

That single visit turned into a mission. We now regularly travel into the most remote and impoverished communities across Cambodia, bringing essential school supplies directly to children who need them most.

The Challenge

Why basic supplies matter so much.

Cambodia has made significant progress in education, but in rural areas, many families survive on less than $2 a day. School supplies are simply unaffordable.

Without pencils and notebooks, children cannot participate in class. Without backpacks, they can't carry materials to school. Without crayons, they lose the creative spark that makes learning joyful.

The result? Children drop out. Dreams fade. Cycles of poverty continue. But it doesn't have to be this way — and that's exactly why we exist.

Our Values

What we stand for.

Our Journey

How we got here.

2024

The First Visit

A small group visited a village near Phnom Penh and donated their first batch of supplies to 30 children.

2024

Word Spreads

Friends and family began donating. We made three more trips, reaching 150 children across Kampong Cham province.

2025

Dream Fund Cambodia Founded

We officially registered as a charitable organisation to expand our reach and build lasting community partnerships.

2025

Expanding Reach

Trips to Battambang, Siem Reap, and Pursat provinces. Over 800 children received full supply packs.

2026

Growing Every Day

With your support, we continue to reach more villages, more children, and more dreams every month.
