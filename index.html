Our Mission

Simple supplies. Extraordinary joy.

In some of Cambodia's most remote villages, children walk miles to reach a school that has no pencils, no paper, no crayons. These aren't luxuries — they're the building blocks of learning.

Dream Fund Cambodia goes directly into these communities with backpacks full of essential school supplies. The joy on a child's face when they hold their first set of coloured pencils — it's something that stays with you forever.

We believe that every child, no matter where they're born, deserves the tools to learn, to dream, and to build a future.

What We Provide

The essentials that spark a future.

Every supply pack is carefully curated to give children everything they need to engage fully in their education.

✏️

Pencils & Pens

Quality writing instruments so every child can put their thoughts on paper and practice their letters.

📓

Notebooks

Fresh exercise books for maths, language, and all the stories waiting to be written.

🎨

Crayons & Colours

Coloured pencils and crayons to unlock creativity and bring joy to every lesson.

📐

Rulers & Erasers

Essential stationery that makes learning possible — the basics we often take for granted.

🎒

Backpacks

Durable bags to carry their supplies and give children a sense of pride on the walk to school.

📚

Books & Readers

Age-appropriate reading materials in Khmer to encourage literacy and a love of learning.
